OpenBee · handlebar navigator

Display simulator

A virtual ride on the 1.28″ round display. The phone plans the route, sends the streets around you and each navigation update over the real Bluetooth protocol, and the screen draws them the way the firmware does. Pick a road, then try a wrong turn or a dropped GPS signal.

Swipe the screen sideways like the touchscreen. Tapping Trip or Status returns to Nav; on Settings, tap − / + to change brightness.

Runs the project's own app/src/core code (nav engine, road-shape extraction, session, protocol v1). The screen is a canvas port of firmware/components/ui/ui.c, so layout and colors match the firmware; the real device renders with LVGL and its own fonts, so expect small differences in text rendering. The side streets here are generated for the demo roads; the app fetches real ones from OpenStreetMap.
